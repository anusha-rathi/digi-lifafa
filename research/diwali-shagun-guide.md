## Persona (Stage 0)

I am 27, I work in Bangalore, my family is in Kanpur. Diwali is the one time
a year money moves around my family in a way I am expected to understand and
nobody has ever explained. Last year I sent my younger cousin ₹2,000 on GPay
and my mother made a face. I want to know the number and the reason, not four
hundred words about the festival of lights. And now that all of it goes over
UPI instead of cash, I have a low-grade worry nobody will say out loud: is
any of this visible to anyone? Does it count as income for the person
receiving it?

## Stage 2 — The open web

Query: "Diwali shagun amount how much money to give" plus reformulations
("Diwali bonus household help maid driver how much India").

Top results read 29 Aug 2026: weddingkart.co glossary, thenyota.app (two
pages), moditoys.com, dnaindia.com, rudraksha-ratna.com, mangalparinay.com,
quora threads.

What they cover, near-unanimously:
- amounts end in one, the extra rupee is the blessing / ashirvaad
- even numbers read as complete or closed, odd as continuing
- rough bands by relationship (colleague ₹501–1,100, friend ₹1,100–2,100,
  close family ₹11,000+) (thenyota.app, read 29 Aug 2026)
- silver coins embossed with Lakshmi or Ganesha as an alternative to cash
- "give what you can" grace note

What NOT ONE of them covers:
- that shagun has a tax treatment at all
- that "relative" is a defined term in the Income Tax Act and cousins are
  outside it
- that the ₹50,000 threshold is a cliff, not a taper
- that UPI made shagun a bank record for the first time in its history

Practitioner voice: Quora threads on Diwali bonus for house help converge on
one month's salary as the norm, with regional variance. Treated as anecdote,
not data.

## Findings, with sources

**Dates (2026)**
- Dhanteras Fri 6 Nov 2026; Lakshmi Puja / main Diwali Sun 8 Nov 2026;
  Govardhan Puja Mon 9 Nov 2026; Bhai Dooj Tue 10 Nov 2026.
  (hindutone.com, muhuratchoghadiya.com, vrindavanmathuratourism.com, read
  29 Aug 2026; three independent sources agree.)
- Lakshmi Puja muhurat commonly given as roughly 18:27–20:27, varies by city.
  Treated as approximate and city-dependent in the post.

**Gift tax, Section 56(2)(x), Income Tax Act 1961**
- Money received without consideration is taxable as Income from Other
  Sources if the AGGREGATE from all non-relatives in a financial year exceeds
  ₹50,000. (taxbuddy.com, bssridhar.com, bcasonline.org, read 29 Aug 2026.)
- Cliff, not a taper: once the aggregate crosses ₹50,000 the FULL amount is
  taxable, not only the excess. (bcasonline.org and taxbuddy.com, read
  29 Aug 2026; two independent sources state it the same way.)
- "Relative" is defined and includes: spouse; siblings and their spouses;
  siblings of either parent (uncles and aunts) and their spouses; lineal
  ascendants and descendants; and the same for the spouse's side.
  (bssridhar.com, chartered accountants, read 29 Aug 2026.)
- **Cousins, nephews and nieces are NOT relatives** under that definition.
  (bssridhar.com, read 29 Aug 2026.)
- The definition is directional, read from the recipient's side. A gift from
  your uncle to you is exempt, because your uncle is your parent's sibling.
  A gift from your nephew to you is not, because a nephew is not on the list.
  (Outlook Business and itatonline.org, read 29 Aug 2026.)
- Wedding gifts to the couple are exempt regardless of value or donor.
  Diwali shagun is not. (taxbuddy.com, bssridhar.com, read 29 Aug 2026.)

**Employer gifts, Rule 3(7)(iv), Income-tax Rules 1962**
- Non-cash gifts, vouchers and hampers from an employer are exempt up to an
  aggregate ₹5,000 per financial year; above that the entire value is a
  taxable perquisite under Salary. (taxtmi.com manual on Rule 3(7)(iv),
  upstox.com, read 29 Aug 2026.)
- A CASH Diwali bonus is not covered by that ₹5,000 exemption. It is salary
  and fully taxable. (outlookmoney.com, taxconcept.net, read 29 Aug 2026.)

**Household help**
- One month's salary is the widely reported norm for a Diwali bonus to
  domestic staff. Practitioner threads only, no survey data located.
  (Quora threads, read 29 Aug 2026. Flagged in the post as custom, not data.)

## Answer in three sentences

Diwali shagun amounts end in one because an even figure reads as closed, and
closed sums are what you hand over at a funeral. What you give depends
almost entirely on relationship and on what is normal in your own family,
with the common bands running ₹101 for a child to ₹1,101 and up for a newly
married couple. The part nobody writes down is that once shagun moves over
UPI it is a bank record, and under Section 56(2)(x) money from people the
Income Tax Act does not count as relatives, cousins included, is taxable in
the recipient's hands once the year's total from all such people passes
₹50,000.

## The gaps we fill

- No ranking page mentions tax at all.
- No ranking page mentions that cousins are outside the statutory definition
  of relative, which is the single most common shagun relationship for
  people in their twenties.
- No ranking page mentions the cliff edge at ₹50,000.
- No ranking page distinguishes a cash Diwali bonus from a non-cash gift for
  the person who employs domestic help.
- No ranking page addresses what changed when shagun moved to UPI.

## Open flags

- The Lakshmi Puja muhurat varies by city and the sources give a generic
  window. The post says so rather than printing one time as if it were
  national.
- No official survey of household-help Diwali bonuses was located. One
  month's salary is reported custom, and the post labels it as such.
- Tax thresholds are stated as at 29 August 2026 and carry a not-advice
  line. They change in Finance Acts and the post must be re-checked yearly.

# What people actually search, and who currently answers

SERP pulled 1 September 2026 via `scripts/serp.sh`, google.co.in, hl=en.
Twelve queries, twelve credits. Raw JSON per query alongside this file.

**On Keyword Planner.** It needs a Google Ads login this project does not
have, and its volumes come bucketed so widely they are close to useless for
choosing between two topics. People Also Ask is better for this job anyway:
those are questions Google has measured people asking straight after the
search, verbatim, and they are what an FAQ block should answer.

## The finding, in one line

Every life-event gifting query returns a shop. Not a thin article, not a
content farm. An actual shop. FNP, Vaaree, Shazé, Amazon, Etsy, Flipkart,
Meesho, Blinkit, Pinterest. Nobody is explaining the custom, because everyone
ranking is selling the object.

## Query by query

Ranks: Reddit r/india, Quora, Vogue India, thenyota.app, and vendors.
**PAA: "Is 3100 a shagun amount?" · "Is 11111 a shagun amount?"**
Related: is 4100 · is 7100 · is 5001 · shagun amount 11000
→ Nothing answers any of them. **Written: `shagun-amounts-which-numbers`.**

### is 3100 a shagun amount
Ranks: a Reddit thread, a Quora thread, and an Instagram post.
→ Confirms the gap directly. No page answers the question asked.

### griha pravesh gift
Ranks: Veda Connection, FNP, Shazé, Vaaree, Cimplifabb, Instagram. All shops.
Related: **gift for griha pravesh under 500** · griha pravesh gift blinkit

### mundan ceremony
Ranks: an invitation printer, Instagram, two baby-product brands, TOI.
**PAA: "Is mundan necessary for a baby girl?"** — nobody answers it.
Related: mundan ceremony PNG · banner · card. Mostly people buying stationery.

### navratri kanya pujan gift
Ranks: Meesho, Flipkart, Pinterest ×2, Wooden Street, BoxUp. All shops.
**PAA: "अगर नौ कन्या ना मिले तो क्या करें?"** — what if you cannot find nine
girls. A real problem, unanswered.
Related: **gifts for Kanya Pujan under 100 · under 50**

### diwali bonus for maid
The one genuinely contested SERP, and the most interesting.
Ranks: Reddit r/mumbai, a Bengaluru Facebook group, Quora, Moneycontrol,
**Economic and Political Weekly**, Times of India.
**PAA is entirely about the STATUTORY bonus**: "Is my bonus 8.33% or 20%?",
"How to calculate Diwali bonus in salary?"
→ The Payment of Bonus Act and a customary Diwali bonus to a domestic worker
are different things and the SERP is conflating them. Nobody separates them.

### bhai dooj tika
Well served, in Hindi, by AajTak, Hindustan, India TV, Drik Panchang.
PAA is all muhurat and which finger. **Nothing about the money at all**,
which is the gap: the nek is the transaction and nobody writes about it.

### karva chauth sargi
Well served in Hindi by Amar Ujala, Hindustan, NDTV, FNP. Competitive.
Gap is narrow: sargi is a gift the saas sends, and it is covered as a menu.

### nazar utarna
Served, folk-remedy register. TOI, Sanatan.org, Reddit r/hinduism.
Related: with salt clockwise or anticlockwise · burning red chillies

## What this changes about the plan

Two kinds of post, and they need different handling.

**Where the SERP is shops** (griha pravesh, baby shower, engagement, mundan,
kanya pujan): write the explanation nobody has written. Low competition,
genuine demand, and it is the site's actual subject.

**Where the SERP is Hindi news** (bhai dooj, karva chauth, navratri dates):
do not compete on muhurat. Amar Ujala will win that every time and should.
Compete on the money, which they never cover.

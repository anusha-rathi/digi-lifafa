wider — five different territories

Not the envelope

The last five were all the same move: draw the object — envelope, coin, letter. That's one axis, and it's the crowded one. Every money-transfer app in India already owns a rounded envelope.

The product isn't about an envelope. That's packaging. It's about a relationship that stays open. So these five come from five unrelated places: arithmetic, thread, gesture, flower, and pure abstraction.

The test is unchanged and it's the honest one: every mark shown at 24px header, on light and dark, and at 32px favicon. Last time my favourite on paper lost badly at 32px, so the small column decides it, not the big one.

1और एक

Plus one

TERRITORY · ARITHMETIC

Shagun is ₹101, ₹501, ₹1,101 — never round. The extra rupee exists so the amount can't be settled, so the relationship carries on. That whole idea compresses to two characters. +1 is legible at any size, in any script, to anyone — and it's a name, a tagline and a mark at once. It's the only one of these that survives being 16px.

The knot

TERRITORY · THREAD

The mauli tied round your wrist, the rakhi, the gathbandhan knot at a wedding. In every case a thread means a bond you don't undo — the same meaning as the unsettled rupee, carried by an object every Indian household already reads instantly. Warmer than a number, and nobody in payments is using it.

3हाथ

The giving hand

TERRITORY · GESTURE

A lifafa is pressed into a hand. Nobody posts one. The product exists because that gesture cannot happen at a distance — so the mark is the thing being replaced, not the thing being sent. The only one here about two people rather than an object. Drawn as cupped palms, symmetric on purpose: my first attempt was a single angled hand and at 24px it read as a rude gesture, which is the kind of thing you only find by shrinking it.

4गेंदा

Marigold

TERRITORY · FLOWER

The flower on every doorway, every garland, every festival in the country. It says "celebration" before anyone reads a word, and it's a circle, which is the shape that survives shrinking. It also hands you an ownable colour — you already have marigold in the palette. The risk: warm and generic; a florist could use it.

5मोड़

The fold

TERRITORY · ABSTRACTION

The most reduced thing that is still true: a single crease. Not an envelope — the act that makes one. A square with one fold and the shadow it casts. Quiet, modern, scales to anything, and the only mark here that would look at home next to a bank's. Cost: it says nothing about India, and this product is not a neutral one.

Where these came from. Arithmetic (the number is the insight), thread (the bond that isn't undone), gesture (the thing being replaced), flower (instant festive recognition), abstraction (the act, not the object). None of them draw the product.

My read: 1. +1 is the only mark that is simultaneously the cultural insight, legible at any size in any script, and impossible to confuse with a mail app. 2 is the one I'd fight for if you want warmth over wit. 5 is the best-made and the least Indian, which is the wrong trade for this product.

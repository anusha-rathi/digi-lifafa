डिजि लिफ़ाफ़ा — logo samples

Right now the logo is type only, Yatra One in maroon, with no mark and no favicon — public/ still holds the Next.js starter SVGs. Each direction below is shown at the three sizes that decide it: the lockup, the 24px header, and a 32px favicon. A mark that dies at 32px is not a mark.

1सिक्का

The one rupee

The strongest idea the product owns. Shagun is never a round number — the extra rupee exists so the account can't be settled and the relationship stays open. A circle survives shrinking better than a silhouette does — but look at the 32px row before believing that: the ₹1 inside it goes muddy, so as a favicon this is a gold dot, not a coin.

The flap

You already own this shape — it is FLAP_PATH from Envelope.tsx, the triple-scallop every lifafa in the app is cut with. Nothing else in the category looks like it. Free consistency: the logo and the product are literally the same curve. Weakest of the five at 32px, though — the scallops dissolve and it becomes a blob.

The monogram

One Devanagari letter — the ल of लिफ़ाफ़ा — foil-stamped in a roundel. Unmistakably Indian, and the least likely to be confused with a mail app. Reads at any size because it's one glyph. The risk: it means nothing to a non-Devanagari reader.

Wordmark only

डि

Two constraints these had to satisfy. Devanagari is never letter-spaced — tracking detaches the matras and डिजि लिफ़ाफ़ा falls apart, so only the Latin subtitles are tracked. And every mark is tested at 32px, because most people meet this product as a browser tab and a WhatsApp preview, not as a hero.

My read, after looking at the 32px row rather than guessing: 3. I expected to land on the coin, and the render says otherwise — at favicon size the coin is a gold dot, the flap dissolves into a blob, and the chain becomes two smudges. ल is the only one still legible, and it is the most ownable of the five. The coin idea is better expressed where it already lives: inside the product, as the last beat of the reveal. Second choice is 2, for the lockup only, with something else carrying the favicon.
